One funny line, at most 200 characters, that lands its laugh on a first read. Free to enter, judged in pairs on surprise, economy, freshness and clean wording.

The entry line (143 characters):

> Mrs. Okonkwo at Flat 9 has knocked on my door every morning since March to borrow sugar; yesterday she knocked to give me some, and I panicked.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline).
